The models and the research behind them

What we start with

Simple models, each backed by published research or a production system.
PARTMODELWHY / WHO USED IT
② HabitBayesian update over written habitsWorks with no data; misconceptions as "wrong rules" (WatChat, MIT research preprint, 2024)
③ SkillLogistic regression + featuresBeats deep models on small/medium data (Gervet 2020, 9 datasets); Duolingo's Birdbrain started as a similar model
④ MemoryHalf-life regression, per personDuolingo HLR (2016); personalized review beat one-size spaced review by 10% on an exam (Lindsey 2014)
⑤ ChooserRules + Thompson sampling on one decisionThompson sampling chose explanations in the AXIS study, tested with 524 people (Williams 2016)
⑥ WordsFast LLM, numbers checked by codeDeep knowledge tracing consistently beat an LLM as the learner model (Hooshyar 2025), so the LLM only writes words
